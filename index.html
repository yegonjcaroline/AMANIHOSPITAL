Welcome to Amani Hospital

We welcome you to Amani Hospital, a faith-based hospital serving with faith and healing with love.

Our dedicated healthcare professionals care for the body, mind and spirit because we believe that caring caring for people goes beyond treating an illness - it means caring for the whole person.

Our Key Services

Outpatient Care

At Amani Hospital, our Outpatient Department provides compassionate, convenient, and quality healthcare for patients who do not require hospital admission. Our dedicated healthcare team offers timely assessment, diagnosis, treatment, follow-up care, and health advice in a welcoming and respectful environment. We are committed to making every visit comfortable and efficient while ensuring that each patient receives personalized attention. Guided by our values of faith, compassion, integrity, and excellence, we care for you and your family with the understanding that every person deserves to be treated with dignity and kindness.

Emergency Care

At Amani Hospital, our Emergency Department is committed to providing prompt and compassionate care when you need it most. Our healthcare team responds to urgent medical conditions with professionalism, efficiency, and compassion. We strive to provide a safe environment where patients and families can receive immediate attention and support during unexpected health emergencies.

Maternity Care

At Amani Hospital, we are committed to supporting mothers and families throughout pregnancy, childbirth, and the postnatal period. Our maternity services are designed to provide compassionate, respectful, and quality care for both mother and baby. From antenatal care to delivery and postnatal support, our team is here to walk with you through this important journey with confidence, care, and hope.

Laboratory

Our laboratory provides essential diagnostic services to support accurate and timely medical decisions. With professional laboratory personnel and appropriate diagnostic procedures, we aim to provide reliable results to assist healthcare providers in diagnosing conditions and monitoring treatment. We are committed to quality, confidentiality, and patient care.

Pharmacy

Our Pharmacy Department provides access to prescribed medicines and professional guidance on their safe and appropriate use. Our pharmacy team works closely with healthcare providers to support effective treatment while helping patients understand their medications, dosage, and instructions. We serve every patient with care, professionalism, and integrity.

Radiology & Diagonistic Imaging

Our Radiology Department provides diagnostic imaging services that assist healthcare professionals in identifying and monitoring various medical conditions. We aim to provide a comfortable patient experience while delivering dependable diagnostic support. Every service is provided with attention to safety, privacy, and compassionate care.

Dental Care

At Amani Hospital, our Dental Department is committed to promoting healthy teeth and gums through preventive, diagnostic, and restorative dental care. We provide a welcoming environment for children, adults, and families while encouraging regular dental check-ups and good oral-health practices.

Health & Wellness

We believe that good healthcare is not only about treating illness but also about promoting healthy living and preventing disease.Our health and wellness services encourage individuals and families to take an active role in their wellbeing through health education, preventive care, regular check-ups, and healthy lifestyle choices.

Specialist & Follo-Up Care

Patients who require additional medical attention can receive appropriate specialist consultations and follow-up care. Our approach focuses on understanding each patient's needs, monitoring their progress, and supporting continuity of care. We work to ensure that patients feel informed, respected, and supported throughout their healthcare journey.
